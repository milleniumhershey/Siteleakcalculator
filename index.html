Where to find your numbers

These Google tools are free. If you have never set them up, that is normal. Most agency owners haven't.

Google Analytics

Shows every visitor to your website. Look at "Users" for the last 28 days. This is the best number for step 1.

Google Business Profile

Your Google Maps listing. Open Performance and look at "Website clicks." This only counts people who came from your listing, so it runs low.

Google Search Console

Shows clicks from Google Search. It may email you a monthly summary once it is set up. It also runs low, because it skips other traffic.

Your phone log or CRM

Count calls and web forms from families asking about care. Do not count caregiver job applicants.
